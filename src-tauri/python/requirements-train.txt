# Training environment (mlx_lm.lora / fuse / generate) — Apple Silicon LoRA training.

# while llama.cpp's GGUF converter pins transformers==4.57.6. Those two cannot

# is how a typosquatted or broken release lands on every Mac at once. Move a
# pin on purpose, then re-run the standing gates.
#
